# Scholarship and Financial Aids at Vietnam Japan University

Vietnam Japan University (VJU), a member of Vietnam National University Hanoi, offers scholarships and financial aid to applicants of its master's programs.

## 1. Master's Programs (2025)
VJU enrolls students in eight master's programs:
- Area Studies Program (Vietnamese Studies or Japanese Studies orientation)
- Business Administration (MBA)
- Climate Change & Development
- Environmental Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Nanotechnology
- Public Policy
- Global Leadership

## 2. Tuition Fees (full 2-year training period)
- Vietnamese students: 92,000,000 VND
- Foreign students: 110,000,000 VND

## 3. JAIF Scholarship (Japan-ASEAN Integration Foundation)
- Coverage: 100% tuition fee (approximately 110 million VND), plus enrollment fee, admission fee, living expenses of 250 USD/month, airfare, and health insurance.
- Eligibility: nationals of Cambodia, Laos, and Myanmar.
- Quota: 10 awards.

## 4. Merit-Based Scholarships
Competitive awards covering 100% or 50% of tuition, funded by partner organisations including Yamamoto, Nippon Steel Vietnam, SEKISHO IKUEIKAI, Nissaku Co. Ltd., and Fast Retailing. The Fast Retailing scholarship additionally includes Japanese language training and internship opportunities.

## 5. Need-Based Assistance
The Principal Scholarship covers 50% of the tuition fee for students facing financial hardship. It is awarded once (non-recurring).

## 6. Support for Vietnamese Students
Vietnamese students receive 100% tuition coverage for the first semester only, including the enrollment fee and admission fee.
